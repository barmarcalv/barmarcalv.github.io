## Domestika — 2021

Domestika is a creative learning marketplace, and it ships on web, Android and iOS at
the same time. I led the design of its cross-platform design system, working directly
with the three engineering teams.

The hard part was not drawing components. It was agreeing on what "the same" means when
a control has to feel native on iOS, native on Android, and correct on the web — and
deciding which differences are worth defending and which are just history.

## Bankinter — 2018 — 2020

Bankinter's design system was built on top of a new brand identity, for web and app, in
a sector where a small inconsistency reads as a small breach of trust.

A bank's interface carries obligations a marketplace does not: legal copy, regulated
flows, error states that have to be exactly right, and a user base that includes people
who will not forgive an interface that changes underneath them.

## The pattern across all three

A design system is only partly a design problem. Most of the work is deciding who gets
to change what, writing the reasoning down, and making the right choice the easy one.
The components are the visible ten per cent.
